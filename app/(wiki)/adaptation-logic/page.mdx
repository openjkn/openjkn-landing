## Critical Functional Gaps Addressed

To bridge these gaps, OpenJKN implements a specialized translation and logic layer to handle uniquely Indonesian requirements:

- **Multi-scheme Premium Collection:** Handles complex segmentation native to BPJS Kesehatan, including PBI (Penerima Bantuan Iuran), PPU, PBPU, and BP.
- **Performance-based Capitation:** Direct integration of Case-mix logic and INA-CBGs payment models, which are central to the Indonesian healthcare payment architecture.
- **Facility Management:** Advanced capabilities for healthcare facility credentialing, dynamic contract management, and bridging virtual claims (V-Claim).
- **Localized Variables:** Support for detailed Indonesian facility types (Faskes Tingkat Pertama & Lanjutan), Mobile JKN appointment systems, and localized doctor scheduling formats.
