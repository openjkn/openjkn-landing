OpenJKN is built as an open-source additional module or plugin on top of the global openIMIS platform. Its primary purpose is not to replace the existing BPJS Kesehatan production system, but rather to serve as a "digital sandbox" and an interoperable "learning laboratory."

## Key Objectives

### Safe Simulation
Creates a secure environment for students and researchers to study JKN logic without risking live production data.

### Policy Innovation
Acts as a medium for policy experimentation and a living laboratory for AI development in the health sector.
